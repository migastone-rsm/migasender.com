How it works

Activate your instance

  1. Purchase the service and wait for the URL to register your WhatsApp number (or numbers).
  2. Forward the QR code to another phone or to a computer and scan the QR code you see on screen with the WhatsApp number you want to connect. If you reached the maximum number of WhatsApp numbers you can register, just remove the ones you don't use anymore.
  3. Finally you get a WhatsApp message and an email with your activation parameters.

Automate your WhatsApp

By purchasing the service you get access to an API, a way to communicate with our service that lets you automate the sending of your WhatsApp messages. To do this you need automation software such as Make or Zapier.

  1. If you aren't an expert, watch the tutorials in this academy, starting from Automation Tutorials EN.
  2. If you are a pro, read the API documentation.
